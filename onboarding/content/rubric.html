Assessment rubric

Score each competency 0–2: 0 missing or incorrect; 1 partial or needs substantial help; 2 independently demonstrated with reproducible evidence. Progress requires 2 on all five gates; a high total cannot compensate for unsafe changes or weak tests.

GateEvidence for 2Retry when incomplete
ReproducibilityFresh isolated reset, exact commits/versions/commands, another person repeats the result.Repair setup and rerun from clean state.
Correct diagnosisTraceable observations separate config, service and test faults; cause explains the symptom.Gather one discriminating observation and revise the hypothesis.
Meaningful testsIndependent expectations, relevant boundaries, mutation rejected, failing-before/passing-after retained.Replace weak checks and prove a bad implementation fails.
Safe reviewed changeBounded diff, preserved contract tags, no unrelated edits, appropriate local tests and exact-head CI verification or accurately documented blocker.Reduce diff, review impact and resolve recoverable failures.
Explanation & handoverClear cause/repair, evidence links, limitations, release boundary, reset and rollback steps.Ask a peer to follow the report and fill specific gaps.

Remote CI blocked by access cannot be marked green. Record it as a delivery gate requiring follow-up. The ownership boundary (tests atop QaaS versus Framework/Runner ownership) must be resolved before claiming broader readiness.
