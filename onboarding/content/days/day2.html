Day 2 — Assertions that detect wrong behavior

Goal: prove that the checker rejects broken behavior. Budget: 6 focused hours.

  1. 0–1h: read the eight-row specification and YAML. Explain what each built-in assertion proves and its limits.
  2. 1–3h: run the assertion regressions. Compare missing/duplicate ID failures with count-only checks. Review why literal fixtures avoid a copied transformation algorithm.
  3. 3–4h: complete starter TODO A: exact age expectations for 24/25, 34/35, 44/45 and 54/55. Avoid deriving expected values with the same rule under test.
  4. 4–5h: in a disposable experiment, mutate one preserved field, one derived field, the title and the timestamp. Capture the specific failing assertion for each and revert the mutation.
  5. 5–6h: explain why a parser accepting protobuf defaults does not prove field correctness; why 5 outputs can hide 1 lost + 1 duplicated record; and why a five-message batch does not prove continuous throughput.
