From reproducible test to safe maintenance

A five-day practical path through OmegaSolider, Mamtzetza and QaaS tests. Start with a working batch, follow one SoldierId, improve the evidence, diagnose faults, and hand over a reviewed change.

Graph update is on hold until the parent confirms that QaaS Shampoo has stopped and identifies its final graph and exam. No original graph or original exam has been incorporated. The practical assessment here is a newly proposed assessment, pending reconciliation.

Plan for roughly 30 focused hours plus 5–10 hours for setup, review and retries. This is a planning estimate assuming existing C#, Git, Docker and HTTP ability; the learner's baseline has not been verified. Move at competency gates rather than the calendar.

Choose the ownership boundary

This week prepares maintenance of an application and its QaaS tests, with existing Runner/Mocker packages. Whether the role also owns QaaS Framework or Runner remains unconfirmed. Framework changes need an additional platform track: hook contracts, protocol/serialization/policy ownership, downstream restore compatibility and reviewed releases. Do not treat this week as certification for framework ownership.
